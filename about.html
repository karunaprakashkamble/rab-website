Who We Are

Welcome to R.A. Bhageria Educational Academy

100%Commitment
to Every Child

At R.A. Bhageria Educational Academy, we are dedicated to nurturing young minds and shaping future leaders. Established with a vision to provide quality education in a holistic environment, our academy combines academic excellence with co-curricular and extra-curricular activities.

We strive to foster creativity, critical thinking, and ethical values in every student, ensuring they grow into responsible, confident, and compassionate individuals. With state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and a supportive learning atmosphere, we aim to empower students to achieve their full potential and prepare them for a successful journey in life.

"Service Before Self"

Our motto since 1990 — guiding every classroom, every activity and every decision we make for our students.

Frequently Asked Questions

Admission is simple: submit an enquiry (online or by calling +91 63581 59567), visit the campus with your child, fill in the admission form, and complete a short interaction with our team. Our office will guide you through documents and fees at every step.

We follow a modern, NEP-aligned curriculum with activity-based learning from Pre-Primary onwards — building strong foundations in languages, mathematics, science and computers, along with art, sports and value education. Please contact the school office for details of the current academic session.

Yes. Safe school van service with trained staff is available, covering Eksal and nearby villages and towns across the Bharuch district. Routes and charges can be confirmed with the school office.
